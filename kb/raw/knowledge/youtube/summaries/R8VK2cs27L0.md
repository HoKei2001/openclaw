# How much does a PATENT COST?

**Channel**: inventRightTV
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R8VK2cs27L0
**Published**: 2023-02-07

---

## Overview

Inventor and licensing expert Stephen Key provides a frank breakdown of the real costs involved in getting a patent, from the initial provisional application through the full non-provisional process. Drawing on his own experience obtaining over 20 patents — including patents he defended in federal court against Lego — he frames patent costs in the context of business strategy rather than purely as a legal expense.

For provisional patent applications, the cost ranges from the $65–$70 USPTO filing fee (DIY) to $1,500–$2,500 if you hire an attorney or agent. Stephen also mentions his own inventRight SmartIP program as a $99 self-guided option. For non-provisional applications, costs vary widely based on complexity, geographic market, and the attorney or agent hired: from roughly $6,000–$8,000 (including office actions) for simpler consumer inventions to up to $30,000 for highly technical or commercially competitive inventions. Stephen's own packaging-industry patents cost around $25,000 each due to the number of claims and the need for strong protection.

A central message is that the quality of the patent depends more on the quality of the information provided to the attorney than on how much money is spent. Stephen delivers a sobering statistic: 97% of patents never recoup their filing costs, which is why he consistently advocates using a provisional application to test market demand first before committing to the expensive non-provisional process.

## Key Takeaways

- Provisional patent application: DIY costs $65–$70 in USPTO fees; hiring an attorney or agent typically costs $1,500–$2,500.
- Non-provisional patent application costs range from $6,000–$8,000 (simple consumer products) to $25,000–$30,000 (complex or competitive fields).
- Solo practitioners and patent agents often charge less than large law firms for equivalent quality work.
- The quality of your patent depends more on the information you provide than on the amount you spend.
- 97% of patents never recoup the cost it took to file them — only patent when market demand is confirmed.
- Use a provisional application as a low-cost market validation tool before committing to the full non-provisional process.
- Providing your attorney with a prototype, competitive market analysis, and marketing materials directly improves patent quality.

## Best For

Inventors and entrepreneurs who want a realistic picture of total patent costs at every stage of the process before deciding how and when to invest in patent protection.
