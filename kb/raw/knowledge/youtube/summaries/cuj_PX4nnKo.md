## Overview

Patent attorney at Trenner Law Firm presents a practical roadmap for achieving patent pending status within 30 days, targeting startup founders and individual inventors who feel intimidated by the patent process. The central message is that a provisional patent application can be filed in two to four weeks (or faster if needed for a trade show or investor pitch), and that the process is more accessible than most inventors realize. The attorney argues that if an inventor can write a solid investor pitch deck, they can provide the invention disclosure needed for a provisional patent application.

The roadmap involves three stages: a structured brain dump that documents every technical detail of the invention — describing the problem it solves, how it works, how it can be built, and how it contributes to novelty in the field; selecting the provisional patent application path (versus the more expensive and time-consuming non-provisional); and filing as quickly as possible so the serial number confirming patent pending status can be used in investor meetings and pitches. The attorney uses a client-facing template built on 25 years of practice that organizes the disclosure into title, background paragraph, and bullet-pointed technical description with figures.

The video emphasizes the strategic value of patent pending status for investor relations: investors view patent pending applications as evidence of serious IP, and it allows inventors to freely disclose their technology in meetings without signing NDAs or concealing information.

## Key Takeaways

- A provisional patent application can achieve patent pending status in 30 days or less with proper preparation
- The invention disclosure should document every technical detail: the problem solved, how it works, how it can be built, and what makes it novel
- Patent examiners want concrete, measurable, technical improvements — avoid jargon and focus on specifics
- For startups and individuals on tight budgets, the provisional patent application is the recommended first step
- The provisional grants 12 months of patent pending status with no USPTO examination, office actions, or additional fees during that period
- Patent pending status signals to investors that the company is building real IP and allows open technology discussions without NDAs
- The filing confirmation serial number is the tangible proof of patent pending status
- A patent attorney's template can simplify the disclosure process; the attorney then handles formal drafting and filing

## Best For

Startup founders and individual inventors who are intimidated by the patent process and want a practical, step-by-step roadmap for achieving patent pending status quickly with professional attorney guidance.
