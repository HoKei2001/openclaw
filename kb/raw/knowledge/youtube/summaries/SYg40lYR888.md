# Invention Prior Art Search for Patents with AI

**Channel**: Crypto Dive
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SYg40lYR888
**Published**: 2025-05-04

---

## Overview

This video documents a real-world experiment using AI tools to conduct a prior art search for a new invention idea — an automatically following robotic stroller with distance control sensors. The presenter describes brainstorming the idea during a walk, dictating technical details into ChatGPT to refine the concept, then asking ChatGPT to generate a structured research prompt designed to search for existing patents and similar products. This prompt was then fed into Google Gemini's deep research feature, which spent roughly 30 minutes analyzing 139 websites and patent documents to produce a comprehensive prior art landscape report.

The presenter highlights Google Gemini's deep research as particularly powerful for this use case, noting it examines more sources and produces more thorough analysis than other free AI research tools such as Perplexity or ChatGPT. The AI-generated report included a feature comparison table across existing products and patents, breaking down which features — such as self-propulsion, follow mode, and distance sensing — were or were not present in existing inventions. The presenter emphasizes that this AI-assisted workflow enables inventors to conduct market and patent landscape research in a fraction of the time a human researcher would require.

A practical caution is offered: inventors intending to file patent applications should be careful about what technical details they disclose publicly in AI prompts, since public disclosure can affect patent rights.

## Key Takeaways

- AI tools can dramatically accelerate prior art and market landscape research for new inventions
- Workflow: dictate invention idea into ChatGPT → generate a structured research prompt → paste into Google Gemini deep research
- Google Gemini's deep research feature analyzes large numbers of websites and patents and produces comprehensive reports
- The AI-generated report can include feature comparison tables across existing products and patents
- This AI-assisted approach provides broader coverage than a single human researcher in a fraction of the time
- Inventors should be cautious about disclosing technical details in AI prompts if they intend to file a patent
- The workflow is accessible and free using consumer AI tools, making it practical for individual inventors
- Results can inform patent filing strategy, revealing which features are truly novel versus already patented

## Best For

Inventors who want to use AI tools to quickly conduct an initial prior art and competitive landscape search before deciding whether and how to file a patent application.
