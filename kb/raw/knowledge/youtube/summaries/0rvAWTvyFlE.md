# Write Your Provisional Patent Application Like This

**Channel**: inventRightTV

**Published**: 2018-08-30

## Overview

This video from inventRightTV features a conversation between host Stephen Key and patent attorney Damon Kelly about the right mindset and approach for writing a provisional patent application (PPA). The core message is that inventors should not try to imitate formal patent language when writing a provisional. Instead, they should write in plain, simple English as if explaining the invention to a very bright six-year-old — using storytelling and clarity rather than legal jargon.

The video warns against a common mistake: inventors read existing patents, attempt to mimic that dense legal language, and end up with a confusing document that misuses patent-specific terms. Since a provisional patent application is informal and does not require the same precise legal structure as a non-provisional, inventors should focus on fully disclosing their invention in a natural, narrative style. The goal is to document the problem, the solution, how the invention works, and its advantages over existing alternatives.

Additional guidance covers the importance of full disclosure — putting everything on paper without holding back — and thinking about variations and future applications of the invention. Inventors tend to over-focus on the single best version of their idea, but including alternative implementations and possible future evolutions makes the provisional much stronger and broader in scope.

## Key Takeaways

- Write a provisional patent application in plain English, not legal patent language.
- Use a storytelling approach: describe the problem, how you came up with the solution, how the invention works, and its advantages.
- Aim for the clarity level of explaining to a gifted six-year-old — simple and methodical.
- Disclose everything fully; you can always trim later but you cannot add new material after filing.
- Include alternative variations of the invention, not just the preferred version.
- Think about how your invention addresses future problems or has future applications.
- Avoid getting into the mindset of drafting formal patent claims at the provisional stage.

## Best For

This video is most useful for first-time inventors who need practical, accessible guidance on how to write a provisional patent application themselves without a legal background.
