## Overview

This brief introductory video features registered patent attorney Scott Thorpe explaining his qualifications and approach to software patent prosecution. He highlights his experience prosecuting software patents, his background in software development, and his cost competitiveness compared to larger coastal law firms.

## Key Takeaways

- Software patents require attorneys who understand both the legal framework and the underlying technology to be prosecuted effectively.
- The presenter has successfully prosecuted hundreds of software patents and teaches national courses to other patent attorneys on software patent protection and patentability.
- Practical software development experience — including work ranging from microcode to database applications — is a key asset for a software patent attorney.
- Boutique or regional patent attorneys may offer significantly lower rates than large coastal law firms while providing comparable or superior technical expertise.

## Topics Covered

- Software patent prosecution experience and qualifications
- Teaching and training other attorneys on software patentability
- Software technology background as a practitioner asset
- Cost advantages of working with non-coastal patent attorneys
- Software invention protection strategies

## Best For

Software companies and individual software developers seeking a technically experienced patent attorney for software patent prosecution, particularly those looking for cost-effective alternatives to large coastal law firms.
