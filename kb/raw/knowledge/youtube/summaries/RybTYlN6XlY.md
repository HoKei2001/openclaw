# Getting a Patent Attorney You Trust: What To Do

## Overview

Stephen Key of inventRightTV delivers a comprehensive set of tips for finding a trustworthy patent attorney and maximizing the quality of a patent application. Speaking from the perspective of a successful inventor who has filed and defended multiple patents, Stephen provides a practical checklist for evaluating and working with patent practitioners. His core premise is that inventors must take an active, informed role — the attorney is only as good as the information and guidance the inventor provides.

The video covers the full workflow of working with a patent attorney: starting with a provisional application to test the market, then preparing thoroughly before moving to the non-provisional. Stephen strongly advises inventors to bring a working prototype (along with honest knowledge of what works and does not), marketing materials that show competitive differentiation, and a clear understanding of similar existing products. This information helps the attorney identify and protect what truly matters.

A particularly valuable section covers claim review: when the attorney drafts the claims, inventors should read them carefully and verify that they protect the specific features identified as important. If the claims are unclear or hard to understand, that is a red flag. Stephen also warns about attorneys who do not disclose the total cost of prosecution including office actions, and recommends asking upfront about billing process, timeline, and who actually does the work.

## Key Takeaways

- Start with a provisional patent application to test market demand before committing to the expensive non-provisional.
- Bring a working prototype to your attorney, along with honest knowledge of what works and what does not.
- Provide marketing materials so your attorney understands the commercial differentiation you need to protect.
- Research competitors and prior art before meeting with your attorney to identify your invention's key point of difference.
- Always ask your attorney for the total cost estimate, including office action responses, from start to finish.
- Read the patent claims your attorney drafts and verify they protect the features that matter most to you.
- If you cannot understand the claims or the application, that is a red flag about the attorney's ability to communicate clearly.
- Know who will actually be doing the work — the senior attorney or a junior associate — and whether that affects quality.

## Best For

Inventors who have already decided to pursue a patent and want practical guidance on selecting a trustworthy attorney and collaborating effectively to produce a high-quality, valuable patent application.
