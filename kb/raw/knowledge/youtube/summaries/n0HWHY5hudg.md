## Overview

This video from Bold Patents Law Firm explains the importance of patent searching before filing and guides inventors through a two-stage search process. The presenter recommends starting with your own preliminary research before hiring a professional search team, emphasizing that investing thousands of dollars in a patent application without first searching could be a costly mistake. The goal is to understand what examiners will find and to gauge the likely scope of patent rights.

## Key Takeaways

- Do your own preliminary research first — spend two to three hours on search engines, public resources, and libraries to check if your idea has been done before.
- After your own diligence, hire a professional search team before filing; skipping this step can waste $10,000 or more.
- Professional searchers look at USPTO databases, published patent applications in the US and foreign countries — the same sources an examiner would use.
- Knowing what an examiner will find in advance helps you determine whether a patent is obtainable and what scope of rights you can expect.
