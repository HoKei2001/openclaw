## Overview

This short but comprehensive overview from Patent PC presents five distinct strategies for monetizing a patent, targeting inventors and business leaders who have filed patents but have not yet converted them into revenue. The video opens with the striking statistic that over 90% of patents are never monetized, framing the content as an urgent call to action for innovators sitting on valuable but dormant intellectual property. The core argument is that patents are not merely legal documents — they are business assets that can attract investors, open partnership doors, and create negotiating leverage.

The five strategies are covered in sequence with balanced treatment of their respective benefits and challenges. Licensing is positioned as the most lucrative and popular route, described as "renting out a property" — the patent owner retains ownership while others pay royalties for access. Outright patent sales provide a faster lump-sum option for inventors who do not want long-term management responsibilities, with Patent PC emphasizing the importance of accurate market valuation. Enforcement against infringers is presented as potentially lucrative but costly, noting that settlements and licenses can result from catching infringing use — though litigation risk must be weighed carefully.

The fourth strategy is building a business around the invention, where the patent becomes the foundation for attracting startup investment. The fifth is participation in patent pools, particularly standard essential patents (SEPs) in areas like 5G, where patents that become part of a technical standard can yield royalty streams through bodies like the IETF. Patent PC is offered as a partner to help inventors navigate all five paths.

## Key Takeaways

- Over 90% of patents are never monetized — understanding your options is the first step to changing that
- Licensing generates steady royalty income without losing ownership and is the most commonly pursued path
- Outright patent sales offer a fast lump sum but require careful valuation; brokers and specialized firms can maximize value
- Patent enforcement through litigation or demand letters can be lucrative but is costly and time-consuming, making it more viable for larger entities
- Building a startup around a patent is a high-risk, high-reward path that benefits from investor funding
- Standard essential patents in industries like 5G can yield royalties through patent pools if the patent is accepted into a formal technical standard
- Professional guidance from firms like Patent PC can help inventors identify which monetization path best fits their situation
