## Overview

UpCounsel provides a structured breakdown of patent costs across all major patent types and at every stage of the process, helping inventors and entrepreneurs budget accurately for IP protection. The video distinguishes between the basic government filing fees (which are relatively low) and the total costs that include attorney fees, drawings, patent searches, and prosecution — which can be substantially higher.

The video covers four main patent categories and their typical cost ranges: provisional patent applications ($65 in basic fees, but $5,000–$9,000 with legal assistance), non-provisional utility patents ($900 in basic fees, but $8,000–$15,000+ with legal support), design patents ($2,500–$3,000 total including legal fees), and plant patents ($4,600–$7,600 total). Costs scale with invention complexity: a simple invention might cost $5,000–$7,000 to patent, while software-related inventions typically start at $16,000, and highly complex technology like MRI scanners may exceed $14,000.

The video also highlights often-overlooked costs: patent prosecution fees for responding to office actions ($1,000–$5,000 per round), professional drawings ($300–$1,000), patentability searches ($1,000–$3,000), international PCT filing ($1,200–$3,000 plus translation and foreign attorney fees), and maintenance fees (paid at 3.5, 7.5, and 11.5 years after grant, ranging from $980 to over $4,000 for small entities). A real-world example of a mobile health app with AI features estimates total patent costs at approximately $21,800.

## Key Takeaways

- Provisional patent: $65 in USPTO fees alone, but typically $5,000–$9,000 with legal assistance.
- Non-provisional utility patent: $900 in basic fees, but $8,000–$15,000+ with attorney representation.
- Design patent total cost with legal fees: $2,500–$3,000.
- Invention complexity drives cost significantly: simple inventions cost $5,000–$7,000; software inventions start at $16,000+.
- Office action responses add $1,000–$5,000 per round to the total cost of prosecution.
- International PCT filing adds $1,200–$3,000 plus translation and local attorney fees in each target country.
- Maintenance fees must be paid at 3.5, 7.5, and 11.5 years after grant to keep a utility patent active.
- Filing a provisional patent first is a cost-effective strategy to secure a priority date while refining the invention.

## Best For

Inventors and entrepreneurs who need a detailed cost breakdown across all patent types to create an accurate budget for IP protection before committing to the process.
