## Overview

This Patent PC video provides a detailed walkthrough of the patent monetization landscape, framing the 90%-never-monetized statistic as evidence that most patent holders lack a strategic plan rather than a valuable invention. The video opens by distinguishing between a patent as a legal document (protection) versus a patent as a business asset (revenue generator), arguing that the mindset shift from one to the other is prerequisite to any monetization success.

Three main monetization pathways are explored. Licensing is described as renting out a property — the patent owner retains ownership while other companies pay royalties for the right to use the technology — and is presented as the most lucrative and commonly pursued route. Patent sales offer a lump-sum exit for holders who prefer not to manage long-term royalty relationships, but the video emphasizes that accurate market valuation is critical to avoid leaving significant money on the table. Enforcement against infringers is presented as potentially high-reward but is flagged as a costly, time-consuming process better suited to larger entities with legal resources to sustain litigation.

The video also introduces two less commonly discussed paths: building a startup or business using the patent as a foundational asset to attract investor funding, and participating in patent pools — particularly relevant for standard essential patents (SEPs) in 5G and other technical standards — where patents accepted into standards bodies like the IETF can yield royalty streams from all companies implementing that standard. Patent PC is positioned throughout as a firm that can help patent holders evaluate all five options and choose the path best suited to their portfolio and goals.

## Key Takeaways

- Over 90% of patents are never monetized; the key gap is a strategic plan rather than a lack of valuable IP
- Licensing (royalties for permission to use) is the most commonly pursued path and preserves ownership while generating recurring income
- Patent sales provide a clean exit via lump sum but require accurate market valuation — mispricing is a primary source of lost value
- Enforcement litigation can be lucrative but is expensive and slow; it favors patent holders with the financial resources to sustain the process
- Building a business around a patent uses the IP to attract startup investors and establish competitive differentiation
- Standard essential patents (SEPs) in technologies like 5G can be contributed to patent pools through bodies like the IETF for royalty income from all implementers
- Professional guidance on patent valuation and strategy selection is recommended before committing to any monetization path

## Best For

Patent holders — from individual inventors to business leaders with IP portfolios — who want a comprehensive map of all five monetization strategies before deciding which path to pursue.
