## Overview

Attorney Elizabeth Potts Weinstein provides a comprehensive, up-to-date walkthrough of filing a trademark application in the United States using the USPTO's new Trademark Center system that launched in 2025. The tutorial covers every step from login and identity verification through submitting payment, and includes all three major application scenarios: intent-to-use (1B), use in commerce (1A), and logo/design mark filings.

## Key Takeaways

- Filing a trademark in the new USPTO Trademark Center system requires a verified USPTO.gov account tied to an ID.me identity verification; applicants outside the United States must have a licensed US attorney file on their behalf
- The filing fee is $350 per international class; using the Trademark ID manual database to identify goods and services (rather than creating custom descriptions) saves money and dramatically increases the likelihood of approval
- Beware of scammers who use the publicly available contact information from your trademark application to send fake invoices and notices — the trademark office communicates exclusively by email, never by text or unsolicited phone call
- For intent-to-use (1B) applications, no specimen is required at filing; for use-in-commerce (1A) applications, a specimen must be submitted showing the trademark being used in connection with the actual product or service — computer-generated mockups are often rejected
- If filing a logo/design mark, the logo must be filed in black and white unless you are claiming specific colors; any change to the logo after filing requires a new application
- The trademark must exactly match how it is used in commerce — punctuation, spacing, and ampersands matter (though capitalization generally does not for word marks)
- After filing, it currently takes at least 6 months before the USPTO examiner contacts you; the serial number is available immediately and can be used for Amazon Brand Registry or other purposes
- Drafts are automatically saved and can be retrieved from the Drafts and Docket section if you lose internet or need to return later
