## Overview

This short video challenges the common perception that software patents are dead, arguing that software inventions can still qualify for patent protection under current rules. The presenter urges technology entrepreneurs to actively file patents rather than assume their code cannot be protected, warning that failing to file leaves them vulnerable to competitors who are actively building patent portfolios.

## Key Takeaways

- Software patents are not dead — there are updated rules under which software code can qualify for patent protection.
- Building technology without filing patents is compared to "building a house on land you don't own" — a significant strategic risk.
- Major technology companies like Google and Microsoft file thousands of patent applications every year, creating a competitive patent landscape.
- Entrepreneurs who do not file patents risk becoming spectators while competitors gain patent ownership over similar technologies.
- The video invites entrepreneurs to consult with the presenter to determine whether their code qualifies for patent protection under the current rules.

## Topics Covered

- Current eligibility of software for patent protection
- Strategic importance of patent filing for technology companies
- Patent filing activity of major tech companies (Google, Microsoft)
- Risks of not filing patents as a technology entrepreneur
- Invitation to consult on software patent eligibility

## Best For

Software developers, technology entrepreneurs, and startup founders who have dismissed software patents as unavailable and need to reconsider their IP strategy in light of current patent eligibility rules.
