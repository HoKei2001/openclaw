## Overview

This video demonstrates IP Author's generative AI-powered patent search platform and its real-world application at Ornite, a company using it to accelerate prior art searches during the invention disclosure review process. The presentation shows how the platform moves beyond traditional keyword and classification code searching by automatically interpreting invention disclosures and producing ranked, explained prior art results — dramatically reducing the time needed to assess patentability.

## Key Takeaways

- IP Author's AI platform eliminates the need for complex keyword gymnastics, class codes, and Boolean searches by interpreting the invention disclosure and automatically identifying the problem being solved, the proposed solution, and the novelty of the invention
- Search results include relevance scores, explanations of how similar or different each prior art reference is to the invention, and highlighted text from each document — providing transparency and reducing reliance on guesswork
- The platform was evaluated by one of Europe's preeminent patent offices alongside other generative AI patent search engines and was identified as the best performing, producing the highest number of X and A references across 20 searches in various technology domains
- IP Author achieves approximately 70% of the quality of human patent searching but in a fraction of the time, making it a powerful tool for first-pass prior art assessment
- About 2/3 of the time, IP Author produced an X reference (highly relevant prior art that could anticipate a claim); A references (relevant prior art) were produced nearly 100% of the time
- At Ornite, prior art searches that previously required a team member working for days to a week can now be completed much faster, allowing the team to move to patent drafting sooner in the invention disclosure workflow
- The platform also supports main claim-based searching, where users can input a proposed claim and have the system search for prior art that could anticipate or render obvious that specific claim; results are structured for inclusion in an IDS filing
