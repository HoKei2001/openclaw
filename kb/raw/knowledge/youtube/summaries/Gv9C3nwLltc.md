## Overview

This technical instructional video from IP Guru covers the formal requirements for filing a non-provisional utility patent application at the USPTO. It begins by explaining the three filing methods: electronic filing via EFS-Web (now called Patent Center), U.S. mail, or hand delivery to the USPTO office. The vast majority of applications are filed electronically, as paper or hand-delivery filings require an additional $400 non-electronic filing fee (reduced to $200 for small entities). This surcharge does not apply to reissue, design, plant, or provisional applications.

The video provides detailed technical requirements for documents submitted via EFS-Web: all files must be in PDF format with specific margin requirements (top and bottom margins at least 2 cm/0.75 inch, left margin at least 2.5 cm/1 inch, right margin at least 2 cm/0.75 inch), standard page size (8.5 x 11 inches or A4), line spacing of 1.5 or double-spaced, non-script font (Arial, Times New Roman, or Courier) at 12-point, and consecutive page numbering centrally located above or below text. Handwritten text scanned to PDF is not acceptable.

The complete application must include, in order: the utility patent application transmittal form or letter, appropriate fees, an application data sheet, the specification (description and claims), drawings when necessary, an executed oath or declaration, and sequence listings or large tables when applicable. Applications in languages other than English must be accompanied by a certified translation and payment of a translation fee.

## Key Takeaways

- Three filing methods: EFS-Web (electronic), U.S. mail, or hand delivery — electronic avoids a $400 surcharge
- Small entities pay only $200 for the non-electronic filing surcharge; micro entities also qualify for reduced fees
- All PDF documents must have specific margins (2cm top/bottom/right, 2.5cm left), 8.5x11 or A4 size, 12-point non-script font, 1.5 or double line spacing
- Required components in order: transmittal form, fees, application data sheet, specification with claims, drawings, oath/declaration, sequence listings
- Applications must be in English or accompanied by a certified translation plus fee
- Handwritten text in PDFs is not acceptable for filing
- EFS-Web accepts documents in PDF format converted from standard word processing software
