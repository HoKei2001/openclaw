## Overview

This recorded webinar from The Patent Guru provides a comprehensive, step-by-step screen-share walkthrough of how to file a non-provisional utility patent application through the USPTO Patent Center as an unregistered (individual) user. The presenter demonstrates in real time how to navigate the USPTO website, upload required documents (specification, figures, application data sheet, oath and declaration), calculate fees, and submit the application. A Q&A session at the end addresses common questions about response timelines, micro entity status, and acceptable file formats.

## Key Takeaways

- Go to USPTO.gov and use the Patent Center tab to file as an unregistered user — no account is required for individual filers.
- Required documents include: the specification (with claims and abstract), figures/drawings (black and white preferred to avoid extra fees), an Application Data Sheet (ADS), and an Oath and Declaration.
- Files must be in PDF or DOCX format — JPEGs cannot be uploaded directly; use a tool like qpdf to convert non-standard PDFs to an acceptable format.
- File names must not contain spaces; use underscores or hyphens instead.
- Fee categories: large entity (full price), small entity (reduced, for companies under 500 employees), and micro entity (further reduced, based on individual income — currently up to $202,563).
- The fee structure covers filing fee, search fee, and examination fee; claims beyond 20 total or beyond 3 independent claims incur additional charges.
- Initial administrative review of a filed application takes about one month; actual examination by a patent examiner typically begins 16–18 months after filing.
- If the inventor is the sole applicant, the applicant section of the ADS can be left blank.
