## Overview

This video is a promotional overview of the Direct Submission Program (DSP) offered by Inventor Process, a company that assists ordinary inventors in bringing products to market through licensing rather than self-manufacturing. The presenter, Edward, argues that licensing is the best path for most inventors because the alternative — building a manufacturing business from scratch — requires millions of dollars, extensive supply chains, marketing agencies, legal teams, and enormous time investment. The DSP offers a structured, step-by-step framework covering patent filing, marketing, and royalty negotiation under one program.

Step one of the DSP focuses on intellectual property protection: the company writes a detailed invention description, creates 3D conceptual illustrations, and files a provisional patent application that gives the invention "patent pending" status immediately. This allows inventors to market their idea openly without fear of theft. Step two shifts to marketing: Inventor Process produces a market research report, narrows down 50 potential licensee companies in the relevant industry, builds a custom informational website for the invention, and runs a postcard mailing campaign to keep decision-makers aware of the product. The emphasis is on consistent outreach and persistence.

The final phase is negotiation support, where Inventor Process leverages its experience to secure the best possible licensing agreement. The company shares in the royalties at 10%, aligning its incentives with the inventor's success. Testimonials from clients are presented to validate the program's effectiveness.

## Key Takeaways

- Licensing a patent through a company like Inventor Process is presented as far more accessible than self-manufacturing, which can cost millions
- Filing a provisional patent application is the essential first step to protect an idea before any marketing begins
- A custom-built website and targeted mailing campaigns to 50 potential companies form the core outreach strategy
- Inventor Process earns 10% of royalties only upon success, aligning their incentives with the inventor's
- Persistence in outreach — repeated contact with decision-makers — is emphasized as the key differentiator between deals that close and those that don't
- Patent pending status enables inventors to discuss and market their ideas without legal risk
- The program positions licensing as a path where the inventor retains ultimate approval over any deal

## Best For

First-time inventors who want a full-service, end-to-end licensing partner and are willing to share royalties in exchange for hands-on support with patent filing, marketing, and negotiation.
