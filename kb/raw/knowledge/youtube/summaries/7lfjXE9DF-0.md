## Overview

This short promotional video from Patent Monetize introduces their platform as a solution to the challenge inventors face after their patent is granted: turning that legal protection into actual revenue. The video observes that while many companies exist to assist with patent filing and prosecution, very few focus on the monetization side — connecting patent holders with global manufacturers and buyers who can commercialize the invention.

The platform works by having inventors register and list their patents, after which the Patent Monetize team performs due diligence: assessing the strength of the patent claims, identifying potential buyers, and building marketing strategies. The service includes handling negotiations, bargaining with potential licensees or buyers, and managing legal compliance throughout the process. The pitch positions Patent Monetize as a full-service partner from registration through deal closure.

## Key Takeaways

- Most companies focus on patent filing and prosecution; few address monetization, which is the gap Patent Monetize targets
- After registration, the platform's team performs due diligence on patent strength and identifies potential buyers globally
- Services include marketing strategy development, buyer negotiation, bargaining, and legal compliance management
- Patent holders retain control while the platform acts as an intermediary connecting them with manufacturers and companies worldwide
- The service is positioned as a turnkey solution for converting a granted patent into revenue without the inventor managing outreach independently

## Best For

Patent holders who have a granted patent and want a managed, end-to-end platform to connect with global buyers and licensees without handling outreach and negotiations themselves.
