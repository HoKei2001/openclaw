## Overview

This is a recording of a European Patent Office (EPO) online seminar held on February 13, 2025, focused on setting up and using the EPO's Online Filing 2.0 platform — the EPO's recommended tool for filing patent applications electronically. The seminar covers both the new Online Filing 2.0 system and the EPO Mailbox feature, which allows patent applicants and representatives to receive EPO communications electronically rather than by post.

The seminar agenda includes a demonstration of how to configure Online Filing 2.0 for new users, covering administration settings, access rights and permissions, adding new users, creating templates and folders, and managing address books. The session also demonstrates the core filing forms: EP 1001 (European patent application), EP 1038, and PCT RO/101 (PCT international application). Additionally, the seminar covers how to import existing data from the older eOLF (electronic Online Filing Legacy) system into Online Filing 2.0, and briefly introduces the API import/export functionality for integration with other internal business systems.

The Mailbox setup section explains the benefits of electronic communication from the EPO, walks through how to configure the Mailbox in the MyEPO Portfolio platform, how to sign in and access it, and how to manually download received communications.

## Key Takeaways

- Online Filing 2.0 is the EPO's current recommended platform for filing European and PCT patent applications electronically.
- The system supports multi-user setups with configurable access rights, roles, and permissions for different team members.
- Templates and folders allow organizations to streamline workflows and organize multiple patent applications efficiently.
- The address book feature stores and manages information for applicants, inventors, and representatives.
- Key filing forms supported: EP 1001 (European patent application), EP 1038, and PCT RO/101 (PCT international application).
- Data from the older eOLF system can be migrated to Online Filing 2.0.
- An API import/export function enables integration with other internal software systems.
- The EPO Mailbox allows electronic receipt of official EPO communications, replacing postal delivery.

## Best For

Patent attorneys, IP professionals, and corporate IP department staff who file European or PCT applications through the EPO and need to set up or transition to Online Filing 2.0 and the EPO Mailbox system.
