## Overview

This short, focused video from The Patent Guru provides a concise introduction to drafting patent claims for a non-provisional patent application. The presenter explains the two main types of claims — system/device claims and method claims — and emphasizes that drafting claims is both an art and a science that takes years to develop. The core advice is to identify the novel features of your invention and connect all claimed elements together.

## Key Takeaways

- Patent claims are required in non-provisional patent applications but not in provisional applications.
- There are two fundamental types of claims: system/device/apparatus claims (covering physical things like systems, devices, modules) and method claims (covering processes and steps).
- The starting point for drafting claims is to identify whether you have a system claim or a method claim, then focus on the novel or most important features of the invention.
- All elements in the claim must be connected to each other — a claim that lists disconnected elements is insufficient.
- Claim drafting is more art than pure science and takes years to master; professional help is strongly recommended for formal filings.
