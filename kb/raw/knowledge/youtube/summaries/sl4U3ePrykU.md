# Which Patent Attorney Should You Hire?

**Channel**: Deepak Malhotra
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sl4U3ePrykU
**Published**: 2016-08-24

---

## Overview

This short promotional video from the Malhotra Law Firm (patentsusa.com) pitches Deepak Malhotra as a patent attorney specifically suited for small businesses, startups, and solo inventors. The video's central argument is that most law firms of comparable stature to Malhotra's background (protecting IP for major tech companies) do not take on small clients, making his firm unusual in actively seeking out and prioritizing smaller companies and startups.

The pitch emphasizes one-on-one personalized service as the differentiating factor for smaller clients, who are often deprioritized or given junior-attorney attention at larger firms. Malhotra's background with large tech company clients is positioned as proof of expertise, while his preference for working with entrepreneurs and startups is framed as a differentiating commitment. The video covers products, ideas, logos, and software as the categories of intellectual property the firm protects.

## Key Takeaways

- Small businesses and startups often struggle to get quality attention from patent law firms that have built their practice around large tech company clients
- Deepak Malhotra's background includes protecting IP for major tech companies, which he applies to smaller clients who would otherwise not have access to that level of expertise
- One-on-one personalized service is the primary value proposition for small business clients at the firm
- Products, software, ideas, and logos all fall under the IP protection services offered
- The video is primarily a firm promotion, not educational content about patent strategy

## Best For

Small business owners and startup founders looking for a boutique patent attorney who explicitly prioritizes smaller clients over large corporate accounts.
