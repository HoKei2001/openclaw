## Overview

Silicon Valley patent attorney John Ferrell provides a detailed walkthrough of how to write a provisional patent application using a downloadable template. He uses a fictitious invention — a system for detecting and removing jitter from video using digital signal processing and AI — as a practical example throughout the tutorial. The core message is that while there are no formal requirements for a provisional application, using a structured template dramatically improves the quality and usefulness of the document.

The video covers each major section of the template: the title (should be descriptive, not generic), the background of the invention (describing the problem being solved), and the detailed description. Particular emphasis is placed on figures, which Ferrell compares to a storyboard — starting with the big picture and progressively narrowing down to the point of novelty. He explains how element reference numbers work and why consistent numbering across figures aids clarity.

Ferrell also makes the case for including an optional patent claim in a provisional application, not as a legal requirement, but as a checklist to ensure all essential elements are described. He closes with a note that after completing the application, inventors should electronically file it through the USPTO Patent Center portal, and encourages viewers to seek professional help if they get stuck.

## Key Takeaways

- A downloadable provisional patent application template is provided; no formal format requirements exist, but a structured approach is strongly recommended.
- Begin with a descriptive, specific title that conveys both what the invention is and what it does.
- Include a "Background of the Invention" section explaining the problem and prior approaches.
- Figures should tell the story of the invention from big picture to point of novelty, with consistent three-digit element reference numbers.
- The written description should walk through each figure element-by-element, explaining what it is, where it comes from, and how it connects to other elements.
- Including an optional patent claim acts as a checklist to confirm all essential elements are adequately described.
- A provisional application is never examined, so it can be informal, but courts may review it later to verify the priority date.
- After completing the written application, electronically file it at the USPTO Patent Center portal.
