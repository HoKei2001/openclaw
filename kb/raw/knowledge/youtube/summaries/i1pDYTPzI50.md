# Video Conference Interviews with Patent Examiners

**Channel**: USPTOvideo
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i1pDYTPzI50
**Published**: 2015-12-17

---

## Overview

This short official USPTO promotional video encourages patent applicants and their attorneys to take advantage of the USPTO's video conference interview capability, which allows face-to-face communication with a patent examiner from any location without the need to travel to the USPTO's offices.

The video's main message is practical convenience: a participant in the video describes the experience as clicking a camera button and immediately being able to see and speak with the examiner, with no travel time, no advance logistics, and the entire conversation completed in approximately one hour. The video also highlights the ability to share and annotate documents in real time during a video conference interview, which is particularly useful for discussing claim amendments and specific patent drawings during the interview.

The video directs viewers to the USPTO's interview practice resources at USPTO.gov/interviewpractice for more information on how to schedule and use video conference interviews. This is a brief awareness and promotional video rather than a technical tutorial.

## Key Takeaways

- The USPTO offers video conference interviews that allow patent applicants and attorneys to meet with examiners without traveling to the USPTO's physical offices.
- Video conference interviews support real-time document sharing and annotation, which is useful for discussing claim language and drawings.
- The process is described as simple: click the camera button and the interview begins immediately.
- Video interviews eliminate travel time and can typically be completed in approximately one hour.
- More information on scheduling and using video conference interviews is available at USPTO.gov/interviewpractice.
- Video interviews are part of the USPTO's broader effort to make examiner communication more accessible and efficient for applicants.

## Best For

Patent applicants and patent attorneys who have not yet used the USPTO's video conference interview option and want to understand why it is a practical and time-saving alternative to in-person interviews.
