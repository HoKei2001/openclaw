## Overview

This video explains the process of filing a provisional patent application electronically through the USPTO Patent Center. It begins by clarifying what a provisional application is: a way to claim an early filing date without needing formal claims or declarations, lasting 12 months and granting "patent pending" status. This allows inventors time to further develop their invention before filing a full non-provisional patent application.

The video covers the preparation of application materials, including a detailed written description of the invention, clear drawings or illustrations, and a cover sheet or application data sheet identifying the filing as provisional. The typical filing fee for small entities is noted as approximately $160. Inventors must create or log into a USPTO.gov account to access Patent Center, and all documents must be in PDF format and meet size requirements (no larger than 50 megabytes).

The video explains what happens after submission: the Patent Center generates an acknowledgment receipt confirming the filing date and providing an application number, which must be kept safe as proof of the priority date. It also reminds inventors that the non-provisional application must be filed within 12 months, that provisional applications cannot be renewed or extended, and that non-English inventions will require translations when filing the non-provisional application.

## Key Takeaways

- A provisional application establishes an early filing date without requiring formal claims or declarations
- The filing gives 12 months of patent pending status to develop and refine the invention
- Application materials include a detailed description, drawings, and a cover sheet (or application data sheet)
- Filing fees are approximately $160 for small entities
- All documents must be in PDF format and meet size requirements for Patent Center upload
- The acknowledgment receipt containing the application number and filing date must be preserved
- Non-provisional applications must be filed within 12 months or the priority date is forfeited
- Provisional applications cannot be renewed or extended
