## Overview

Dr. Rahul Dev from the Business Tech Innovation Academy breaks down the three fee components that determine the total cost of filing an international patent application through the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT). Understanding these three components — the transmittal fee, the international filing fee, and the search fee — is essential for accurately budgeting PCT filing before taking the international patent route.

The transmittal fee is paid to the receiving office (e.g., USPTO, EPO, or the applicant's national office) and functions like a local service fee to initiate the application. In the United States, this fee is approximately $265, with reductions available for online filing. The selection of receiving office depends on the nationality of the applicant or inventor. The international filing fee is a WIPO fee of approximately $1,500 base, with electronic filing reductions of $100–$200. Applications longer than 30 pages trigger additional per-sheet fees, so keeping applications under 30 pages is cost-effective — though the presenter notes that strong patent drafting (aligned with USPTO, EPO, and JPO requirements) should take priority over page count.

The largest variable cost is typically the search fee paid to the chosen International Searching Authority (ISA). Fees vary significantly by ISA: the European Patent Office charges approximately $1,845, while the United States ISA charges approximately $2,400. Micro entities may qualify for up to 80% discounts in certain situations. The total filing cost is the sum of all three fees, with the final amount depending on the number of pages, entity status, receiving office, search authority selection, and currency conversion rates on the date of filing.

## Key Takeaways

- PCT filing has three fee components: transmittal fee (~$265 for USPTO), international filing fee (~$1,500), and search fee (largest variable cost)
- The European Patent Office charges ~$1,845 as ISA; the USPTO ISA charges ~$2,400 — choose ISA strategically
- Micro entities may qualify for up to 80% discount on some PCT fees
- Electronic filing of the PCT application reduces the international filing fee by $100–$200
- Keeping the application under 30 pages avoids per-sheet fees — but quality drafting for major offices (USPTO, EPO, JPO) takes priority
- The receiving office selection depends on the nationality of the applicant or inventor
- Final total cost depends on entity status, number of pages, currency conversion rates, and choice of receiving office and ISA
