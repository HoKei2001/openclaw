If you’re planning an interview with a patent
examiner or you think an interview might be useful,
A TC Interview specialist like me is just
one of the resources available for your interview.
I’ll be covering resources to help you make
the most of your interview experience.
USPTO.gov/interviewpractice is the home for
all the resources related to Interview Practice.
In our Training and Frequently Asked Questions
section, you’ll find resources such as our
Interview Best Practices guide, and learn
about tools like USPTO AIR and public interview
rooms.
It’s worth your time to take a look and
use these materials to get the most out of
your interview.
And remember, while video conferencing interview
with a patent examiner is fast and easy, first
you must provide us with proper authorization.
The policy and guidance section at USPTO.gov/interview
practice explains the rules and provides guidelines
for Internet communication, from the MPEP.
Applicants must submit the internet authorization
form to permit examiners to email you about
your application.
You can submit your internet authorization
via EFS Web, central fax, US postal service, and the USPTO Customer
Service Window; however, submitting
authorization by email is not compliant with
our current policy.
For a simple and fast way to provide your
authorization, check out the SB/439 form located
on our website.
After visiting USPTO.gov/interviewpractice,
you should have the tools needed to make the
most of your interview experience.
If you still have questions, or you want to
practice video conferencing, our webpage lists
interview specialists like me that you can
call for assistance.
