patent application process Step 1 prior
art search
the first step to patenting an invention
is a prior art or patent search
a prior art search is researched to find
all the products and inventions similar
to an invention that you want to patent
in order for an invention to be
patentable it must be novel or new and
non-obvious
so a patent search is done to determine
if the invention is found in any
existing patents patent applications and
non-patent literature from around the
world
