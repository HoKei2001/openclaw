Excuse me, I've been trying to

Oh, do you know

AIR lets you propose an interview
time with examiners – whether
you are trying to meet online
using video conferencing, over
the phone, or actually coming
into a USPTO office. You go
online, it's a simple process,
just say when you'd like to meet
and click submit. An examiner
emails you to confirm your
request within 2 business days.
No more phone tag.
That'll definitely save me time, and I
like being able to schedule an
interview at my convenience.
To get to AIR visit USPTO.gov/InterviewPractice. And since
we've been talking, I just
requested an interview time
through AIR, quick and easy!
