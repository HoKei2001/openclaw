what is a software patent a software
patent is a property right that protects
computer programs unlike software
copyright which protects the expression
of an idea a software patent protects
the invention itself in the United
States software is considered patentable
if it meets certain criteria to be
eligible for a software patent the
program must have a commercial or
industrial application it should be new
unique and not obvious to others in the
industry it's worth noting that
copyright protection for software is
automatic in 151 countries under the
burn Convention as such any software has
automatic copyright protection in these
countries this protection lasts for your
entire life and potentially up to 70
years after your death however patent
protection requires a separate
application in each desired country
alternatively you can pursue
International protection through the
patent cooperation treaty Define your
software before you can apply for a

protection for you some questions you
can ask to help with this include what's
the overall purpose of my software what

like what elements of it do I want to
