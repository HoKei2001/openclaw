Welcome to Kenton Teaches. Enjoy the
video. In this video, I will explain how
to file a design patent yourself. First,
understand what a design patent
protects. Then, gather your necessary
drawings and specifications.
Next, prepare all application forms
accurately. Finally, submit your
complete application to the United
States Patent and Trademark Office.
Preparing your application requires
careful attention to detail. Your
drawings are the most important element,
so ensure they clearly illustrate the
design from every angle. Write a brief,
accurate description of the design's
nature and intended use. You will also
need to complete an application data
sheet and an inventor's oath or
declaration. Remember, a design patent
application includes only one claim.
Once your application is ready, you'll
submit it electronically through the
USPTO EFS web portal. You will need to
pay the required filing, search, and
examination fees, which vary based on
your entity status, such as micro or
small entity. Double check that all
documents are uploaded correctly before
finalizing. Upon submission, you will
receive an application serial number and
a filing date. After filing, your
application enters the USPTO review
process. Be prepared to respond promptly
to any office actions, which are common.
You can monitor your application status
online using the patent application
information retrieval or pair system.
While self- filing is possible, consider
seeking legal advice for complex
responses. Once granted, a design patent
lasts for 15 years from its issue date
with no maintenance fees. Thanks for
watching. Please subscribe if this video
was helpful.
