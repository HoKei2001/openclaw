Here's a question that every inventor
asks before filing an international
patent. How much will my PCT patent
actually cost? Let's break down the
three hidden components that determine
your total PCT patent filing fee. First
is the transmitt fee. Think of it as
your local travel agency service fee to
start your application. This is paid to
your chosen receiving office, and the
cost varies dramatically depending on
where you file. For example, in the
United States, the fee may range around
$265.
Some offices even offer reductions for
online filing. The decision to select
the appropriate patent office as the
receiving office depends on the
nationality of the patent applicant or
the patent inventor. Next is the
international filing fee. The base fee
is around $1,500,
but there exists an electronic filing
fee reduction that ranges between $100
to $200. Herein the applications longer
than 30 pages trigger an additional fee
per sheet. It makes financial sense to
keep the number of pages to less than
30. But for strong patent drafting, it
is advisable to ensure that the claims
and description are drafted as per the
requirements of major global patent
offices like the USPTO, EPO, and JO.
Finally, the search fee, which is often
your biggest variable cost. You have to
choose an international searching
authority from your receiving offices
approved list, and fees differ
dramatically based on their expertise
and your entity status. The European
Patent Office charges $1,845
while the United States ISA charges
$2,400,
but micro entities can avail up to 80%
discount in certain situations.
Therefore, to determine your total
filing cost estimates, add your
transmitt fee, international filing fee,
and search fee together. Here, it is
important to note that the total fee
will depend on the final contents of the
patent application, including the number
of pages, the filing entity status,
which may be an individual inventor or a
legal entity. the receiving office, the
search authority, and the currency
conversion rates on the date of filing
of application. If you have any doubts
