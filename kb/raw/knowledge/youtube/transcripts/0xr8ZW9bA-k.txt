Welcome to patentproctor.ai,
a revolutionary AI powered platform that
turn your ideas into patents in minutes.
To commence, click the get started
button to sign up and begin creating
your patent draft.
Afterwards, click the continue with
Google button to begin setup with
Google.
Else, click the create account manually
button to begin setup without Google.
Then click the check box to agree to the
terms and privacy policy.
Click the create account button to
complete your registration.
To draft a patent, click start a new
patent draft button to begin creating
your patent application.
Now, enter your first invention details
in the text field to begin the
disclosure process.
Following this, click the send button to
submit your invention description to the
AI assistant.
Following that, enter your second
invention details in the text field to
proceed.
Enter your third invention details in
the text field to continue.
Click generate provisional patent to
create patent documentation for your
invention.
View and edit file. Invention draft can
be enhanced using editing tools as
required.
Patent AI assistant can be used to
enhance or generate sentences.
Click the patentability button to
analyze your patent draft for potential
issues.
Following this, click analyze patent
draft to get AI powered insights on your
patent's patentability.
Review the overall score and
patentability analysis on the panel.
Scroll down to see more analysis.
After preparing the document, click the
save document button to store your
patent draft.
Click the file patent draft button to
file your completed patent application.
From here, enter the inventor's full
details.
Next up, click the next button to
proceed to the next step of your patent
application.
Following that, you can click copy from
inventors to populate applicant details
from existing inventor information or
else fill it up manually.
Click the next button to proceed to the
final step of the application.
From here, select a plan as required.
Now, click proceed to payment button to
complete filing your patent application.
Finally, after drafting and review, you
can manage your application from the
dashboard.
For prior art search, click the start
searching button to begin your prior art
patent search.
Enter your search terms in the patent
database search field to find relevant
prior art.
Click view full details to access the
complete patent information and
specifications.

Click the view source button to access
the original patent document source.
To conclude, after filing patent, status
is visible here.
