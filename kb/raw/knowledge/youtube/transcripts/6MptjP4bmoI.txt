How to file a patent application in
European patent organization?
Patent journey of EPO began in 1973.
The European Patent Organization
protects inventions across Albania,
Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia,
Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark,
Estonia, Finland, France, Germany,
Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland,
Italy, Latvia, Likenstein, Lithuania,
Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro,
Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway,
Poland, Portugal, Romania, San Marino,
Serbia, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden,
Slovenia, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey,
United Kingdom acting as a powerful
shield for innovation.
You have 31 months time to file a PCT
national phase application in EPO.
In EPO, patent application must be in
English, French or German. Translation
is required if the initial language is
not one of these.
No extension for patent filing is
permitted.
There is no document required to file a
patent application in EPO.
EPO gives 30% off on filing and exam
fees if you're an individual or
university from an EPO country.
A request for examination is
automatically filed at the time of
filing a patent application.
The initial renewal fee is due at the
end of the third year from the patent
date. Subsequent renewal fees are
required before the expiration of each
succeeding year.
