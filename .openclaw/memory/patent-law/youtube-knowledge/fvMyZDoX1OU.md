## Overview

This video is a live session from inventRightTV featuring the co-founder of InventRight and their director of project management discussing the significant changes that have occurred at the USPTO regarding provisional patent application filing. The session addresses how the electronic filing process has become dramatically more complicated in recent years, causing many inventors to struggle with simply getting their applications submitted successfully. The presenters share what they've learned from working with clients and students who encountered difficulties with the newer patent office requirements.

The discussion covers what has changed at the USPTO and why filing has become harder, where inventors are most commonly getting stuck in the process, and the approach InventRight currently recommends to successfully navigate the new system. Importantly, the presenters explain why they currently advise against inventors filing electronically on their own and outline a clear step-by-step road map for filing with confidence. The video is framed as an inventor-to-inventor discussion rather than legal advice.

The session also addresses the fundamental importance of provisional patent applications as tools for inventors: they are one of the most important steps in moving an idea forward, and filing one correctly is critical. The presenters draw on real-world experiences from the InventRight community to illustrate common pitfalls and solutions.

## Key Takeaways

- The USPTO provisional patent filing process has become significantly more complicated recently, creating new challenges for inventors filing on their own

- Provisional patent applications remain one of the most important tools inventors have for moving ideas forward
