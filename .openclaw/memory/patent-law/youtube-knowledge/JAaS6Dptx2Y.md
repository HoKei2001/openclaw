## Overview

This USPTO Quick Clinic Series video provides an introduction to two core electronic systems for patent applicants: EFS-Web (the Electronic Filing System) and PAIR (Patent Application Information Retrieval). While EFS-Web has since been replaced by Patent Center, the underlying concepts and the PAIR system remain relevant for understanding how USPTO electronic filings and status tracking work.

EFS-Web allowed applicants to submit patent applications electronically from any internet-connected computer, with the benefit of immediate document routing to USPTO internal systems, instant acknowledgment receipts, and reduced manual processing compared to paper mail. To use the registered (full-access) version of EFS-Web, applicants needed to first obtain a Customer Number (CN) by filing Form SB-125, and then create a verified USPTO.gov account by completing and notarizing Form PTO-2042a and mailing it to the USPTO (processing takes approximately 5–7 business days).

The PAIR system has two components: Public PAIR (accessible to anyone, allowing viewing of published applications and issued patents) and Private PAIR (for registered applicants to track the status of their own pending applications). The video walks through the full electronic submission workflow and explains the two-step authentication process for accessing secure USPTO systems.

## Key Takeaways

- EFS-Web (now replaced by Patent Center) allowed electronic submission of patent applications from any computer, providing immediate routing and confirmation receipts.
- To access the registered/private features, applicants must obtain a Customer Number (CN) via Form SB-125.
- A verified USPTO.gov account requires completing and notarizing Form PTO-2042a and mailing it to the USPTO; processing takes 5–7 business days.
- Public PAIR allows anyone to view published patent applications and issued patents.
- Private PAIR allows registered applicants to monitor the real-time status of their own pending applications.
- The filing workflow: select application type, enter applicant data, attach documents, calculate fees, submit, and receive an acknowledgment receipt with application number.
- The USPTO Electronic Business Center (EBC) is available Monday–Friday 6am–midnight Eastern Time for filing and system support.

## Best For

This video is most useful for first-time filers who need to understand the USPTO's electronic application and status-tracking infrastructure before submitting their first patent application online.
