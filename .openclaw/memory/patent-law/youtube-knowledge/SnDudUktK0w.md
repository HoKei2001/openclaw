## Overview

This brief video from Dave Korpi introduces a 3D modeling service for inventors who need patent drawings for provisional or full patent applications. The core service offering is the creation of SolidWorks-based 3D models from inventor specifications, with deliverables that include USPTO-compliant line drawings (formatted and annotated for direct use in patent applications), the SolidWorks solid part files, IGES and STEP files, and STL files for 3D printing or manufacturing.

The video highlights a key advantage of 3D modeling over traditional 2D illustration: the same model used for patent drawings can also be used directly for manufacturing or prototyping, making it a dual-purpose investment. The resulting drawings are formatted for pasting into a Word document and converting to PDF for USPTO submission, with annotations following standard patent drawing conventions.

## Key Takeaways

- 3D modeling produces patent drawings that can also be used directly for manufacturing or 3D printing, making it a more versatile investment than 2D illustration
- SolidWorks-based drawings meet USPTO formatting and annotation standards for use in both provisional and full patent applications
- Deliverables include the 3D solid part files (IGES, STEP, STL) in addition to the patent-ready drawings
- Drawings are formatted for direct copy-paste into a Word document and conversion to PDF for USPTO submission
- The service is a freelance offering for inventors who need professional drawings without the cost of a full patent attorney

## Best For

Inventors preparing a provisional or full patent application who want professional, USPTO-compliant drawings that can also double as manufacturing-ready 3D model files.
