## Overview

This video provides a concise, step-by-step walkthrough of the provisional patent application process with the USPTO. It explains the fundamental purpose of a provisional application: to secure an early filing date for an invention while giving the inventor 12 months to further develop the idea and prepare a formal non-provisional application. The video emphasizes that provisional applications are less formal than non-provisional ones, requiring no claims or inventor declarations.

The preparation process covers the need for a thorough written description of the invention, detailed drawings illustrating all aspects, a list of all inventors and their contributions, and a description clear enough for someone skilled in the relevant field to understand and replicate the invention. Filing is done electronically through the USPTO's EFS Web system, where inventors complete a cover sheet, upload supporting documents, and pay the required filing fee, which varies by entity status (micro, small, or large).

After filing, the inventor receives an official filing receipt with a serial number. The provisional application remains confidential for its entire 12-month lifespan, during which the inventor can mark the invention as "patent pending." The video stresses that a non-provisional application must be filed within one year to preserve the priority date.

## Key Takeaways

- A provisional patent application secures an early filing date without requiring formal claims or inventor declarations
- The application gives inventors 12 months to develop their idea further before filing a non-provisional application
- Descriptions must be detailed enough for someone skilled in the field to understand and replicate the invention
- Filing is done electronically through USPTO's EFS Web system with a cover sheet, specification, drawings, and fee
- Filing fees vary by entity type: micro, small, or large entity
- After filing, inventors receive an official receipt with a serial number confirming their priority date
- The application remains confidential for its entire 12-month lifespan
- A non-provisional application must be filed within 12 months or the priority date is lost
