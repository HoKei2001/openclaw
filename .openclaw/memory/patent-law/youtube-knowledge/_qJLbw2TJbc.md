## Overview

This video presents a practical, DIY approach to patent searching using four free online methods, demonstrated in real time with a real invention (an anti-splash back device for home toilets). The presenter walks through each method, showing how to leverage Google search, Google Shopping, Google Patents, ChatGPT, and the USPTO website to assess whether an idea is new and novel. The video also provides motivational advice to inventors who may encounter discouraging prior art during their search.

## Key Takeaways

- Start with a basic Google search using product-specific keywords to find both prior art and competing products already on the market.
- Use Google Shopping results alongside standard search results to see what is currently selling — commercial existence matters as much as patents.
- Google Patents is a great starting point for digging into patented material; sort by date and explore related inventors to broaden perspective.
- ChatGPT is more useful for comparing existing patent PDFs and extracting key information than for discovering new prior art, because its training data has a cutoff date.
- The USPTO website is a useful final step for deeper research once you've identified keywords and relevant patents from the other three methods.
- Don't get discouraged if you find similar prior art — differences in application or execution can still leave room for a patentable invention.
