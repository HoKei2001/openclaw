## Overview

This Pinch Patent Drawings video covers the most critical USPTO patent drawing requirements as of 2025, framed as a practical guide to avoiding the common errors that cause rejections, delays, and drawing corrections. The video is structured as a fast-paced overview of the key rules across paper/format specifications, line quality, labeling, view types, and special cases.

On the question of when drawings are required, the video clarifies that drawings are only legally mandatory when necessary to understand the invention — but in practice, almost all applications benefit from them, and when an examiner determines drawings are needed after filing, the USPTO typically allows at least two months to provide them. Diagrams, flowcharts, and block diagrams all qualify as drawings. The format requirements are specific: A4 or US letter white non-shiny sheets, margins of at least 2.5 cm on top and left, 1.5 cm on the right, and 1 cm on the bottom, with no borders or frames. Drawings must be on separate sheets from text. Sheets are numbered with Arabic numerals at the top center; figures are labeled sequentially (Figure 1, Figure 2, etc.) and identified with "new sheet" or "replacement sheet" labels when added or revised.

Line quality rules require black, durable, consistent lines — no colors or grayscale — with even hatching for cross-sections that never obscures reference labels. Text in drawings must be horizontal, at least 0.32 cm tall, and used only when necessary to explain the invention. Reference numbers must match between drawings and the written description, with each part receiving the same number consistently across all figures. Scale must be shown with a graphic bar, not text. Photographs are permitted only for special cases (plants, biological specimens, gels). Freehand sketches are not accepted — CAD or drafting tools are required.

## Key Takeaways

- Drawings are legally required only if necessary to understand the invention, but almost all applications benefit from them; missing sheets cannot be added after filing
- Sheet format: A4 or US letter, white non-shiny paper, margins of 2.5 cm top/left, 1.5 cm right, 1 cm bottom, no borders or frames
- Drawings must be on separate sheets from written description and abstract; sheets numbered at top center with Arabic numerals
- Lines must be black, durable, and consistent — no colors or grayscale; cross-section hatching must be even and must not obscure labels
- Text in drawings must be horizontal, at least 0.32 cm tall, and kept minimal; reference numbers must match the written description throughout
- Scale should be shown with a graphic bar, not text (e.g., "1:2" is not acceptable)
- Photographs are permitted only for special cases like plants, biological specimens, or gels — not for standard mechanical or electronic inventions
