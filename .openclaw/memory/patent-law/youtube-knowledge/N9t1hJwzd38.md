## Overview

Inventor and student Cliff Weitzman demonstrates how to file a provisional patent application in approximately 20 minutes for just $65 (at micro entity rates). The video is explicitly targeted at students and inventors with limited budgets who want to protect their ideas quickly without hiring an attorney. Weitzman spent considerable time navigating the USPTO's online portal and pre-filled all required forms, which he made available via Dropbox to reduce the filing process from what would otherwise take hours.

The video explains the rationale for filing a provisional: the U.S. switched to a first-to-file system, making it more important than ever to establish a filing date early, even before a prototype is built. A provisional application is informal — no formal claims are required, drawings can be sketches or photos, and the filing establishes "patent pending" status for one year. Micro entity status (available to applicants earning under approximately $150,000 per year) provides a 75% fee discount, reducing the filing fee to $65.

The required documents for the provisional application are: the specification (title, field of invention, related art description, drawing descriptions, detailed description, optional claims, and abstract); drawings (any format); the SB/16 cover sheet; and the SB/15A micro entity declaration. These are uploaded through the USPTO's online portal as a new utility/provisional application. The video walks through each screen of the portal and confirms that the whole process takes about 20 minutes using the pre-filled templates.

## Key Takeaways

- A provisional patent can be filed for $65 at micro entity rates (for individuals earning under approximately $150,000/year).
- The provisional establishes "patent pending" status and locks in a one-year priority date under the U.S. first-to-file system.
- No working prototype is required; a clear description and rough drawings are sufficient.
- Required documents: specification, drawings, SB/16 cover sheet, and SB/15A micro entity declaration.
- Drawings for a provisional can be sketches, photographs, or CAD models — informal formats are accepted.
- The USPTO online portal submission takes approximately 20 minutes using pre-filled form templates.
- The provisional must be converted to a full utility patent application within one year to maintain priority.
- Do a basic Google Patents keyword search before filing to confirm no one has already patented the same idea.

## Best For

This video is most useful for students and budget-constrained inventors who want the fastest, cheapest way to establish patent pending status and protect an idea while they continue developing it.
