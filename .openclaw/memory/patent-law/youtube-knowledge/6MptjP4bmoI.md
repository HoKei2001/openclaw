## Overview

This video provides a concise guide to filing a patent application at the European Patent Office (EPO). It covers the EPO's member states, language requirements, filing deadlines, fee structures, and the automatic examination request feature, giving applicants a practical overview of the European patent process.

## Key Takeaways

- The European Patent Organization protects inventions across 39 member states, including major European countries such as Germany, France, the UK, Italy, Spain, and many others.
- Applicants entering the national phase at the EPO via PCT have 31 months from the priority date to file.
- Applications must be filed in English, French, or German; translation is required if the original language is not one of these three official languages.
- No extension for patent filing deadlines is permitted at the EPO.
- No documents are required to initiate a patent application filing at the EPO.
- Individuals and universities from EPO member countries receive a 30% discount on filing and examination fees.
- A request for examination is automatically filed at the time the patent application is submitted — no separate examination request is needed.
- The first renewal fee is due at the end of the third year from the filing date; subsequent annual renewal fees are due before the expiration of each following year.
