## Overview

This concise video walkthrough from File a Patent Online demonstrates the current process for filing a provisional patent application through the USPTO Patent Center website. The presenter walks through the live filing interface, starting from the USPTO.gov homepage, navigating to Patent Center, logging in with a verified account, and selecting the utility provisional application type.

The demo covers the key filing steps: entering the invention title (recommended to be short and generic), selecting entity status (micro entity is demonstrated with a warning about qualifying requirements), providing inventor name and mailing address, and uploading four files — the micro entity certification form, drawings, specification, and cover sheet. The video emphasizes that cover sheets are required for provisional patent applications and that the micro entity fee checkbox must be explicitly checked before payment or the filing will be incomplete.

After uploading, the presenter advises thoroughly previewing all uploaded documents to catch any missing pages, formula errors, or corrupt characters. The final step is clicking the payment button, which is distinct from the submit button — failing to complete payment means the patent office does not have a valid filing. A free template for writing the provisional application is offered on the presenter's website.

## Key Takeaways

- Access Patent Center at patentcenter.uspto.gov and log in with your verified USPTO account (ID.me required).
- Select "New Submission" then "Utility Provisional" to begin the provisional patent application process.
- Cover sheets are required for provisional applications; the presenter skips the ADS and uploads the cover sheet as a separate file instead.
- Entity status must be selected carefully — micro entity filing fees are reduced but require qualification (see warning displayed by the system).
- Upload four files: micro entity certification, drawings, specification, and cover sheet; label each file type correctly.
- After uploading, use the preview function to verify all files appear correctly before submitting.
- After clicking submit, you must also click a separate button to actually pay the filing fees — the filing is not valid without payment.
- The micro entity filing fee for a provisional application is $65 at the time of the video.
