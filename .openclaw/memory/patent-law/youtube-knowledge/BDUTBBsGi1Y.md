# Addition of inventors: Scope and procedure

## Overview

This video addresses the legal provisions under the Indian Patents Act for adding inventors to a patent application. The presenter explains common scenarios that necessitate adding inventors, such as when converting a provisional specification to a complete specification where new contributors emerged, when a contributor's involvement was recognized late, or when someone disputes their exclusion from the inventor list.

The video walks through two primary legal mechanisms for adding inventors. The first is Section 10(6), which allows new inventors to be added at the time of filing the complete specification if they contributed after the provisional filing. The second is Section 28, which provides a post-filing procedure for adding inventors before a patent is granted, requiring a formal request on Form 8, payment of fees, and satisfying the controller that the person genuinely contributed to the invention.

The presenter also discusses how Section 28 allows removal of incorrectly added inventors and emphasizes that natural justice principles apply throughout, with both sides given an opportunity to present their case. The video concludes that the provision allows the inventor's name to be added throughout the entire patent records, not just the certificate, reflecting the moral right of inventors to be recognized.

## Key Takeaways

- Section 10(6) allows additional inventors to be added when converting a provisional to a complete specification, using Form 5
- Section 28 provides the mechanism for adding inventors after the complete specification is filed but before patent grant
- Requests under Section 28 must be made on Form 8 with the requisite fee and evidence of inventorship
- The controller must be satisfied that the person made a genuine contribution to the invention or a substantial part of it
- Disputed inventorship claims (Section 28(3)) allow a claimant to file independently without the applicant's cooperation
- The addition of inventors cannot be requested after the patent is granted (Section 28(4))
- Inventors also have the right to have wrongly added names removed under Section 28(7)
- The provision protects the moral right of inventors to be named, enhancing their professional standing and economic worth
