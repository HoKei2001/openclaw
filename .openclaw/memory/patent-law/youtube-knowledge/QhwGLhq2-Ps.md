## Overview

Silicon Valley patent attorney John Ferrell presents a fully updated 2026 walkthrough for filing a provisional patent application through the USPTO Patent Center. This video was created specifically because the USPTO eliminated guest filing in September 2025, making his earlier popular tutorial obsolete. The demo uses a real 10-page provisional application — "Systems and Methods for Detecting and Removing Jitter from a Video" — as a practical filing example.

The most significant change covered is the new account registration requirement. Inventors must now create a verified USPTO account through a three-step process: verifying identity via ID.me (approximately 30 minutes, requiring government-issued ID), creating a USPTO.gov account, and self-enrolling in Patent Center to receive a customer identification number. The Application Data Sheet (ADS/SB-16) is then completed online through the Web ADS tool, covering inventor information, correspondence address, title of invention, and entity status. The signature section — easily missed — requires typing your full legal name between two forward slashes.

Inventors who qualify for micro-entity status (income under $251,190, fewer than four previously filed non-provisional applications, no assignment to a high-income entity) must upload a completed SB-15-A declaration form. After uploading all documents, the "View Feedback Document" feature helps identify formatting errors before final submission. The video demonstrates how to navigate fee calculation by entity size and completes the process through payment.

## Key Takeaways

- Since September 2025, USPTO Patent Center requires a verified account — guest filing is no longer available.
- Account setup requires ID.me identity verification (approximately 30 minutes) and results in a customer identification number.
- The Application Data Sheet (ADS/SB-16) is filled out online and serves as the cover letter for the application.
- The signature section requires /Full Legal Name/ format using forward slashes — not backward slashes.
- Micro-entity filers must upload the SB-15-A certification form; income threshold as of recording was $251,190.
- Use the "View Feedback Document" feature after uploading to catch formatting errors (e.g., unsupported fonts).
- Claims are not required in a provisional, but including one is useful for later reference when converting to non-provisional.
- Firefox or Chrome are recommended over Safari for compatibility with the Patent Center website.
