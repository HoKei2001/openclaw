# How to choose a Patent Attorney - what makes a good Patent Attorney?

**Channel**: Davies Collison Cave
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_bLbwa_jxo
**Published**: 2015-05-14

---

## Overview

This brief clip from Davies Collison Cave (DCC) features a client describing what characterizes a productive relationship with a patent attorney. The speaker emphasizes that a good patent attorney does more than document an invention — they actively engage with its potential, raising questions about adjacent applications in different industries (medical, aerospace, etc.) that the inventor may not have considered. This expansive thinking is framed as a key marker of attorney quality and a catalyst for broader patent portfolio thinking.

The speaker also describes their personal workflow with their DCC attorney: they prepare a PowerPoint presentation covering the technology and its potential applications, share it with the attorney in advance, and then discuss it by phone. Notably, this PowerPoint-based disclosure often becomes part of the initial patent pending application itself — demonstrating a practical and efficient approach to invention disclosure that bridges the gap between an inventor's natural communication style and the documentation needs of a patent filing.

## Key Takeaways

- A good patent attorney proactively raises adjacent application possibilities in other industries, not just the inventor's primary use case
- Starting with a PowerPoint presentation of the technology is an effective and practical format for initial invention disclosure
- The PowerPoint presentation can serve double duty — as a discussion document and as input to the initial patent pending application
- Building a trusting, collaborative relationship with a patent attorney unlocks better outcomes than a purely transactional document-drafting arrangement
- Cross-industry thinking from the attorney (medical, aerospace, etc.) can expand the scope and value of a patent portfolio

## Best For

Inventors who want a brief illustration of what a proactive, relationship-driven patent attorney engagement looks like in practice.
