# Top 5 Patent Mistakes

**Channel**: Alder IP
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vQcWu9mmAk
**Published**: 2021-06-01

---

## Overview

Anthony Alder of Alder IP (an Australian IP firm) presents the top five patent mistakes he encounters from entrepreneurs and inventors. The video is particularly notable for its international perspective, including specific references to Australian patent law and practice alongside general principles. The presenter uses vivid examples from his experience — including a single pen sale in Australia that constituted a prior disclosure even though no one knew how the pen worked — to illustrate how patent rights can be destroyed by seemingly minor actions.

The five mistakes are: (1) disclosing the invention before filing, which destroys novelty and tips off competitors before patent protection is in place; (2) selling the invention before filing, which counts as prior disclosure and bars patent rights; (3) filing a poor provisional application — the presenter advises that a provisional should be 20 to 40 pages of detailed description, and that anything less is likely insufficient to support a later non-provisional; (4) not doing research (prior art search and market research) before filing or commercializing; and (5) not working with a patent attorney to draft the provisional, which the presenter argues is ultimately far more costly than the money saved, citing multiple medical device companies that lost hundreds of millions in IP value by filing their own provisionals.

## Key Takeaways

- Disclosing an invention before filing destroys novelty and also reveals the invention before getting patent rights
- Selling an invention before filing counts as prior disclosure, barring future patent protection
- Even a single sale can constitute prior disclosure in Australia, even without revealing how the invention works
- A provisional application should be 20 to 40 pages of detailed description to adequately support a later non-provisional
- A one-paragraph provisional will likely be rejected in five years when the non-provisional is examined
- Always do prior art research and market research before filing or commercializing an invention
- Patent attorneys specializing in patents can perform a confidential search on a provisional in Australia
- Working with a patent attorney for provisional drafting is essential — multiple medical device companies lost hundreds of millions in IP by not doing so

## Best For

Entrepreneurs and inventors, particularly those operating in Australia, who want a frank assessment of the most costly patent mistakes and why quality provisional drafting by a professional is worth the investment.
