# How to accelerate the Euro-PCT procedure

**Channel**: European Patent Office
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8omGAABGpcs
**Published**: 2022-08-02

---

## Overview

This short informational video from the European Patent Office (EPO) outlines four concrete steps that applicants can take to significantly shorten the time it takes for a Euro-PCT application (a PCT application entering the European regional phase) to be granted. Under standard processing, a Euro-PCT application can take approximately six years from filing to grant. However, by strategically combining available procedural options, applicants can save up to 30 months off that timeline.

The four acceleration mechanisms covered are: (1) selecting the EPO as the International Searching Authority (ISA) when filing the PCT application, which allows the EPO to bypass the Supplementary European Search in the European phase, saving the six-month response period that typically follows; (2) requesting early entry into the European phase, which can advance the start of examination by more than a year compared to the standard 31-month deadline; (3) waiving the right to further amend the application, which eliminates a six-month waiting period; and (4) filing a PACE (Program for Accelerated Prosecution of European Patents) request, which is free of charge and moves the application to the front of the examination queue.

The video is presented as a concise reference guide from the EPO, using a numbered list format to highlight these time-saving options clearly.

## Key Takeaways

- A standard Euro-PCT application can take approximately six years to be granted; up to 30 months can be saved through acceleration options.
- Selecting the EPO as the PCT International Searching Authority (ISA) bypasses the Supplementary European Search and the associated six-month response window.
- Early entry into the European phase can advance examination by more than one year.
- Waiving the right to further amend the application saves approximately six months.
- Filing a PACE request for accelerated examination is free of charge.
- All four acceleration measures can be used independently or in combination depending on timing needs.
- These options are particularly valuable for applicants who need European patent protection quickly to respond to market competition.

## Best For

This video is most useful for patent attorneys and applicants who are prosecuting Euro-PCT applications and want to understand the specific procedural options available to speed up the grant timeline at the European Patent Office.
