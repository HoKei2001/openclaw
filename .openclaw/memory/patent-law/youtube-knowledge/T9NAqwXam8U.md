## Overview

This short video from Patent Attorney Worldwide, presented by Prasad Karhad, introduces the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) and its purpose in international patent filing. The video establishes the foundational principle that patents are territorial — a patent granted in one country (e.g., the United States) does not prevent others from making, using, or selling the invention in other countries. To protect an invention in multiple jurisdictions, separate patent protection is required in each country or region.

The PCT is described as an international treaty administered by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) with over 150 contracting states. Its primary objective is to simplify international patent filing by allowing a single application that is then searched by at least one International Search Authority (ISA) and optionally examined by an International Preliminary Examination Authority (IPEA). Importantly, the PCT does not grant patents directly — final patent grants remain under the control of the national or regional patent offices in what is called the "national phase." The video primarily serves as a pointer to more detailed country-specific resources available on the Patent Attorney Worldwide website.

## Key Takeaways

- Patents are territorial — a US patent provides no protection in other countries; separate filings are needed in each jurisdiction
- The PCT is an international treaty with 150+ contracting states, administered by WIPO, allowing a single application to initiate protection in multiple countries
- The PCT provides a unified filing and search mechanism but does not grant patents — national phase entry at each country's patent office is still required for a grant
- An International Search Authority (ISA) searches the application for prior art; an optional International Preliminary Examination Authority (IPEA) can provide a patentability assessment
- Country-specific PCT guidance is available for US, India, Europe, UK, Canada, Japan, and Australia through the Patent Attorney Worldwide website
