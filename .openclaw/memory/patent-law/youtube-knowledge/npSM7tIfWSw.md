## Overview

This official USPTO webinar is designed to prepare prospective candidates for the patent examiner hiring process. Led by USPTO HR Specialist Marvin and Supervisory Patent Examiner Peter, the session walks through the four stages of the hiring timeline, the format of both interview rounds, and best practices for performing well throughout the process.

The hiring process consists of four stages. After the application cut-off date passes, the Office of Human Resources reviews applications and may invite qualified candidates to complete a first-round HireVue on-demand video interview. This is an asynchronous format, meaning candidates record responses on their own schedule within a specified window. Each question allows up to five minutes of recording time, and candidates receive two attempts per question — with the second attempt being the one sent to hiring officials. Candidates who advance then receive an invitation to a second-round virtual interview: a 20-minute live session with a senior executive from the Patents organization that includes behavioral questions and time for the candidate to ask questions. The overall timeline from application to hiring decision is approximately 10 weeks, though this varies.

The second half of the session, presented by Peter, focuses on interview best practices. For virtual interviews: test technology in advance, look into the camera to simulate eye contact, dress professionally, maintain a clean and distraction-free background, and avoid camera filters or custom virtual backgrounds. For answering questions: use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure responses, draw on specific work examples relevant to the job description, and avoid reading from scripted answers. The qualities of a good patent examiner — self-starter, goal-oriented, and strong written and oral communication skills — should be woven into responses.

## Key Takeaways

- The USPTO patent examiner hiring process has four stages and typically takes approximately 10 weeks from the application cut-off date.
- The first-round interview is an asynchronous HireVue video interview completed within a scheduled window; each response is limited to five minutes, with two recording attempts available.
- The second-round interview is a 20-minute live virtual session with a senior Patents executive, including time for candidate questions.
- Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure behavioral interview responses clearly and concisely.
- Test your camera, microphone, and platform familiarity before each interview round; look into the camera while speaking.
- Dress professionally and ensure your background is clean and free of visual distractions.
- Key qualities of a strong patent examiner candidate: self-starter, goal-oriented, and strong written and oral communication skills.

## Best For

Prospective candidates with STEM backgrounds who have applied or are considering applying for a USPTO patent examiner role and want to understand the interview process and how to prepare effectively.
