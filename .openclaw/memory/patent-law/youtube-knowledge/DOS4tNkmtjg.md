# How Do I Find a Reliable Patent Attorney?

**Published**: 2025-02-12

## Overview

Bold Patents founder and patent attorney J.D. Houvener answers one of the most common questions from inventors: how do you find a patent attorney you can actually trust? The video frames the entire process as an interview — every time you speak with a patent attorney or law firm, you are evaluating whether they are the right fit for your situation, not just accepting their services.

The video covers three main areas to evaluate. First, personal and firm fit: consider not just the individual attorney but the whole firm, including the staff and working style. Some inventors need in-person relationships while others are comfortable with fully virtual representation. Billing structure is a critical factor — understand upfront whether the firm charges flat fees or bills hourly, and what the total cost is likely to be. Second, reputation: do not just take the attorney at their word. Actively look up reviews on public platforms, and if possible, speak with current or former clients to get firsthand accounts of the firm's reliability and quality of work. Third, accountability: once you begin working with an attorney, set checkpoints to verify that promised milestones are being met. If the attorney is underdelivering, do not hesitate to end the relationship — your time and your invention's value are too important to accept poor service.

## Key Takeaways

- Treat every conversation with a patent attorney as a job interview — you are evaluating fit, not just accepting a service.
- Consider the full law firm, not just the individual attorney; you will likely work with the broader team as well.
- Clarify the fee structure upfront: flat fees vs. hourly billing, and what the total expected cost will be.
- Actively research reputation: look up public reviews and speak with current or former clients.
- Assess fit for your specific needs — virtual vs. in-person, startup-friendly vs. large corporate focus.
- Set checkpoints throughout the engagement to verify that promised deliverables are being met.
- If an attorney misses deadlines or underperforms, do not hesitate to find a replacement.

## Best For

This video is most useful for inventors in the early stages of seeking patent protection who need a practical framework for evaluating and selecting a patent attorney that fits their technical, financial, and working-style needs.
