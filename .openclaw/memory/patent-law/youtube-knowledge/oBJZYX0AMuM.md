## Overview

This video from Bold Patents Law Firm's J.D. Houvener outlines the seven-step process for patenting a mobile application, with particular emphasis on the unique challenges software patents present compared to physical inventions. The video is aimed at app developers and tech entrepreneurs who may be moonlighting on a project and want to understand the patent process before engaging an attorney.

The first step is clarifying inventorship and ownership — before anything else, inventors should review their employment contracts to confirm that their employer cannot claim ownership of the invention. Because software development almost always involves collaboration, co-inventors need to be identified and brought into the process early. The second step is researching other apps to confirm the invention is genuinely novel, which is the foundational requirement for patent protection. The third step is finding a software patent attorney who has both legal training and a technical software background — someone who understands GUIs, virtual reality interfaces, and modern software architecture.

Step four is the invention disclosure: the inventor explains the technical core of the application to the attorney in detail, a process that can take one to two weeks because fully articulating how an app works is more involved than it appears. Step five is a professional patent search conducted by the attorney's team, covering worldwide patents and non-patent literature including academic papers, social media, and Kickstarter. Step six involves filing either a provisional or non-provisional application; for rapidly evolving software technologies, the attorney may recommend skipping the provisional and filing a non-provisional application directly to lock in the filing date as soon as possible. The final step is submitting all required legal documents — the application data sheet, inventor declaration, and oath — properly formatted to the USPTO.

## Key Takeaways

- Review your employment contract before filing — many companies have agreements that give them ownership of inventions created by employees, even off-hours.
- Identify all co-inventors early; software development teams commonly have multiple people with inventive contributions that must be reflected in the application.
- A software patent attorney must understand both law and modern software technology, including user interfaces and emerging tech domains.
- The invention disclosure process takes time because inventors must fully explain the technical "secret sauce" to their attorney.
- For fast-moving software technologies, filing a non-provisional application directly (skipping the provisional) may be recommended to lock in the filing date sooner.
- A professional patent search covers worldwide patent databases and non-patent literature including anything the USPTO examiner might later find.
- The filing requires formal documents including an application data sheet, inventor declaration, and oath — not just a specification.

## Best For

App developers, software entrepreneurs, and startup founders who want to understand the full process for protecting a mobile or software application with a patent before engaging a patent attorney.
