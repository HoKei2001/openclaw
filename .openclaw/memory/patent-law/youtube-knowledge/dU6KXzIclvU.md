## Overview

This brief video from the European Patent Office introduces the European Patent Register, an online public database that provides access to all publicly available information on European patent applications as they progress through the grant procedure, including oppositions and patent attorney correspondence.

## Key Takeaways

- The European Patent Register is a publicly accessible online tool containing comprehensive information on all European patent applications.
- It tracks applications through all stages of the grant procedure, including oppositions filed after grant.
- The register includes correspondence between patent attorneys and the EPO, making the prosecution history publicly available.
- It also provides a public file inspection service, allowing anyone to view documents in a patent file.
- More information is available on the EPO's official website.
