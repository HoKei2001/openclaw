## Overview

Novel Patent Services, a patent services firm with over 18 years of experience and a team of 150+ experts, presents nine practical strategies to help independent inventors reduce patent filing costs without compromising the quality of protection. The video is structured as a numbered tips guide, covering everything from leveraging USPTO fee discounts to limiting international filings.

The most impactful cost-saving strategy is qualifying for USPTO small entity or micro entity status. Small entities (businesses under 500 employees, individual inventors, nonprofits) pay 50% of standard fees. Micro entities — inventors whose gross income is below $184,116 and who have not been named on more than four prior applications — pay only 25% of standard fees. Filing electronically via the USPTO Patent Center avoids an additional $400 paper filing surcharge, and using DOCX format (from 2024 onward) prevents further fees. A provisional patent application can be filed for as little as $70, providing 12 months of patent-pending status.

Other tips include conducting a prior art search using free tools like Google Patents or the USPTO Public Search before drafting the application (to avoid rejections and unnecessary resubmissions), focusing claims on essential features only (to reduce office actions), keeping applications under 30 pages to avoid per-sheet fees, and limiting international filings to countries where commercialization or licensing is actually planned. The video also recommends using free USPTO tools (EFS-Web, PAIR, Patent Public Search) rather than paying for third-party platforms.

## Key Takeaways

- Qualify for micro entity status (75% discount) or small entity status (50% discount) before filing — income threshold is currently ~$184,116
- File electronically via USPTO Patent Center to avoid the $400 paper filing surcharge; use DOCX format from 2024 onward
- A provisional patent application costs as little as $70 and provides 12 months of patent-pending status
- Focus patent claims on essential features only — broader, simpler claims reduce the likelihood of expensive office actions
- Conduct a free prior art search using Google Patents or USPTO Public Search before drafting to avoid rejections
- Limit international filings to countries where you actually plan to commercialize or license your invention
- Use free USPTO tools (EFS-Web, PAIR, Patent Public Search) instead of paid third-party services

## Best For

Independent inventors and startup founders who want to minimize patent filing costs through strategic use of USPTO fee discounts, electronic filing, and targeted claim drafting.
