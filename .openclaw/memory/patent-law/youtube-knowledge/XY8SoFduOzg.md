# What Are The Best Prior Art Search Tools For Patents?

**Published**: 2025-10-26

---

## Overview

This video provides a practical survey of the best tools available for conducting patent prior art searches, covering both traditional patent databases and a new generation of AI-powered search platforms. The presenter explains that a thorough prior art search is an essential step before filing a patent application — it helps inventors assess whether their invention is novel, understand the competitive landscape, and anticipate what objections a USPTO examiner might raise.

For free, publicly available databases, the video highlights Google Patents as the most accessible starting point, praising its clean interface and powerful search capabilities. The USPTO's own patent database (patents.google.com and USPTO full-text databases) and the European Patent Office's Espacenet are also recommended for their comprehensive international coverage. WIPO's PatentScope is identified as the best tool for searching PCT international applications and patents from non-English-speaking countries.

The video then surveys a range of AI-powered patent search tools that have emerged to address the limitations of keyword-only searches. Tools discussed include IP Rally, Ambersite, Novelty, Patitics, Derwent Innovation (a professional-grade Thomson Reuters product), and Dialogue. These AI tools can surface semantically relevant patents even when different terminology is used, making them especially useful for inventors whose concepts may be described in multiple ways. The presenter notes that professional searchers and patent attorneys often combine multiple tools to ensure comprehensive coverage.

## Key Takeaways

- Google Patents is the most accessible free tool for starting a prior art search, with robust search filters.
- The USPTO's patent database and Espacenet (EPO) offer comprehensive international patent coverage at no cost.
- WIPO PatentScope is the best resource for searching PCT international applications.
- AI-powered tools like IP Rally, Ambersite, Novelty, and Patitics can find semantically similar patents that keyword searches miss.
- Derwent Innovation is a professional-grade tool used by patent attorneys and corporate IP teams.
- No single tool is comprehensive — thorough searches combine multiple databases.
- A prior art search before filing helps inventors avoid wasting money on applications likely to be rejected.
- Patent attorneys often conduct or commission professional searches before drafting a non-provisional application.

## Best For

Inventors, entrepreneurs, and IP professionals who want to understand which free and paid tools are available for conducting patent prior art searches before or during the application process.
