# How to Patent Your Invention Idea

**Channel**: inventRightTV
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thUuck_rAUc
**Published**: 2022-01-10

---

## Overview

This inventRightTV video by Stephen Key, co-founder of inventRight, outlines a market-first approach to patent protection for inventors. The core advice is to slow down, resist rushing to file a non-provisional application, and instead use a provisional patent application as an affordable, flexible tool to gather information, test the market, and build a stronger case for protection over time.

Key argues that the provisional patent application (PPA) is the right starting point because it gives inventors 12 months of patent pending status while allowing them to continue developing the invention and discovering new aspects. Multiple PPAs can even be filed during that year and combined before the first one expires. The most important pre-filing step is knowing your point of difference — using Google image search, Google shopping search, and prior patent searches to map out what already exists and confirm what is genuinely new. Key is emphatic that this research cannot be skipped.

For the content of the PPA itself, Key recommends going beyond a simple description of the invention. The application should describe the problem the invention solves in plain language, the solution, and the inventor's point of difference. Critically, it should also include manufacturing details and material information — information that requires research but dramatically increases the application's value to a potential licensee or investor. Finally, Key advises including variations and workarounds — actively imagining how a competitor might copy the concept differently, then including those alternatives in the provisional to protect the broader innovation, not just the specific embodiment. Detailed line drawings (not just sketches) with labeled components are essential for capturing everything that words might miss.

## Key Takeaways

- File a provisional patent application first — do not rush into a non-provisional before fully researching the invention and the market.
- Use Google image search, Google shopping search, and Google Patents to identify similar products and prior patents and confirm your point of difference.
- A provisional application can be filed multiple times during a year, and multiple PPAs can be combined before the first one's expiration date.
- Include a clear description of the problem and solution in plain language, along with your specific point of difference versus existing products and patents.
- Adding manufacturing details and material information requires extra research but is what makes a provisional application truly valuable to licensees and investors.
- Include workarounds and variations ("steal it from yourself") to protect the broader innovation, not just your specific implementation.
- Detailed line drawings with labeled components capture visual information that text often misses and cover variations that might otherwise be unprotected.

## Best For

Inventors at the earliest stage of protecting a consumer product idea who want a practical, market-aware approach to patent filing that maximizes the value of the provisional application before investing in a full non-provisional.
