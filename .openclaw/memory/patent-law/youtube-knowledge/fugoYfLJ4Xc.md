## Overview

This short video from the "File a Patent Online" channel explains the basic structure of a patent document and walks through the process of filing a provisional patent application, with a focus on how to minimize upfront costs. The video breaks the patent document into three main components: the written description (specification), the drawings or figures, and the cover sheet for provisional applications.

The specification is described as having seven sections: title, background, brief summary, brief description of figures, detailed description (the most important and longest section), optional claims, and an abstract. For software or app inventions, the video recommends using flowcharts as figures. The channel provides a free downloadable template on its website (patentfile.org). Filing is done through USPTO.gov via the Patent Center portal.

The video also addresses the concept of filing "for free" by not paying the fee at the time of submission. While technically possible — the patent office will send a notice with a surcharge — this approach is explicitly discouraged. The filing fee should always be paid at the time of submission to avoid additional costs and complications.

## Key Takeaways

- A patent document has three main parts: the written specification, drawings/figures, and the provisional cover sheet.
- The specification has seven sections, with the detailed description being the most critical and should be at least a couple of pages long.
- Claims are optional for a provisional patent application; the abstract should be 150 words or less.
- Figures should clearly label all important components; for software inventions, flowcharts are an effective substitute for physical drawings.
- Filing is done at USPTO.gov via Patent Center — New Submission — Utility Provisional.
- You can technically file without paying the fee, but a surcharge will be added and it is strongly discouraged.
- A free specification template is available at patentfile.org.
- Save all documents as separate PDF files before uploading.
