# How to get a DESIGN PATENT

**Channel**: Vanader
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xy21WTlst7s
**Published**: 2022-01-27

## Overview

This video from the Vanader "Brick by Brick" channel documents a real, first-person experience filing two design patents for a vape case product using a patent attorney found on Fiverr. The video is unusual in its transparency — showing actual Fiverr conversation screenshots, itemized cost breakdowns, the finished application documents, and the solid-line drawings submitted to the USPTO. The goal is to demystify the design patent process and demonstrate that it can be accessible and affordable for small business owners.

The cost breakdown is detailed: $65 for a patent search, $250 for the design patent work, $150 for application drafting, $30 per drawing (optional), plus $255 per patent in USPTO fees (micro-entity rate) for a total of approximately $980 in attorney fees and $510 in USPTO fees — roughly $1,500 for two complete design patents. The presenter emphasizes providing the attorney with CAD files upfront for the best results, and recommends faxing payment forms to the USPTO rather than mailing them to avoid processing delays.

The application structure is illustrated directly: five drawings (front, bottom, side, complete, and perspective views) with solid lines defining what is claimed, and a single one-sentence claim ("the ornamental design for the vape case as shown and described"). The presenter acknowledges they used a holding company as the named inventor on the patent to protect personal assets, and notes they will report back once the examiner's review is complete before fully endorsing the Fiverr approach for serious patent work.

## Key Takeaways

- Filing two design patents through a Fiverr patent attorney cost approximately $1,500 total ($980 attorney fees + $510 USPTO fees at micro-entity rate)
- The design patent application consists primarily of drawings; the claim is a single standardized sentence
- Solid lines in design patent drawings define what is claimed; dotted lines and gradient outlines define context and unclaimed elements
- Providing CAD files to the attorney upfront results in a faster, cleaner process than starting from scratch
- USPTO payment should be faxed rather than mailed to avoid processing delays that result in late fees
- Naming a holding company (rather than an individual) as patent owner is a common asset protection strategy
- The examiner's review and outcome will determine whether a low-cost Fiverr approach produces adequate patent strength

## Best For

Entrepreneurs and small business owners who want a transparent, real-cost walkthrough of filing a design patent through a budget-conscious approach using a Fiverr patent attorney.
