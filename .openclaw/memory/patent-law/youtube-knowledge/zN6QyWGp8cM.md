## Overview

This short USPTO instructional video introduces the Automated Interview Request (AIR) tool, which allows patent applicants and attorneys to schedule interviews with patent examiners online without the frustration of playing phone tag. The video explains how to use the tool and what support resources are available.

## Key Takeaways

- The USPTO's AIR (Automated Interview Request) tool allows applicants to propose an interview time with a patent examiner online, eliminating the need for back-and-forth phone calls.
- Interviews can be scheduled for video conferencing, phone calls, or in-person meetings at a USPTO office.
- The process is simple: go online, select a proposed time, and click submit; the examiner emails a confirmation within 2 business days.
- AIR is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from anywhere in the world.
- Interview Specialists at the USPTO are available for questions about interview practice and policy, and can provide one-on-one video conferencing training.
- The tool is accessible at USPTO.gov/InterviewPractice.
