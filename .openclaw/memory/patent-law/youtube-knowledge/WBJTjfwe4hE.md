## Overview

This is a recording of a European Patent Office (EPO) online seminar from March 19, 2024, focused on setting up and using EPO Online Filing 2.0 — the EPO's recommended tool for electronic patent filing, replacing the older eOLF system. The seminar provides a comprehensive walkthrough of the system's setup and administrative features for both new and transitioning users.

The seminar covers the full configuration workflow for Online Filing 2.0: setting up administrative access, managing user permissions and roles, adding new team members, creating filing templates and folders for workflow organization, and managing the address book for applicants and representatives. The session then demonstrates filing the three main EPO application forms: EP 1001 (standard European patent application), EP 1038, and PCT RO/101 (PCT international application filed through the EPO as receiving office). Information on importing data from the legacy eOLF system is provided to ease the transition, along with a brief introduction to the API functionality for connecting Online Filing 2.0 to other internal systems.

The video serves as a practical onboarding resource for patent practitioners and corporate IP teams who need to adopt Online Filing 2.0 as their primary EPO filing platform.

## Key Takeaways

- Online Filing 2.0 is the EPO's current tool of choice for electronic patent filing, replacing the older eOLF system.
- The system supports multi-user setups with configurable access rights and permission levels.
- Templates and folders help organize multiple patent applications and standardize filing workflows.
- Address book management stores and reuses applicant, inventor, and representative contact information.
- Supported filing forms: EP 1001 (European patent application), EP 1038, and PCT RO/101.
- Data from the legacy eOLF system can be imported into Online Filing 2.0 for continuity.
- An API import/export feature supports integration with other internal IP management systems.
- The seminar is geared toward new users and those transitioning from eOLF.

## Best For

Patent attorneys, IP managers, and corporate IP department staff who file European or PCT patent applications and need to onboard or migrate to EPO's Online Filing 2.0 system.
