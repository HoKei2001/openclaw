# PPA Structure Template

## 1. Title (≤500 chars)

## 2. Field of Invention

Brief statement of the technical field.

## 3. Background

- Describe the problem or need
- Cite relevant prior art (from Prior Art Report)
- Explain why existing solutions are inadequate

## 4. Summary of the Invention

- Core innovation in 1-2 paragraphs
- Broadest possible claim scope

## 5. Brief Description of Drawings

- List each figure and what it shows

## 6. Detailed Description of Preferred Embodiments

- Main embodiment (most complete)
- Alternative embodiments (broadens protection)
- Reference numerals matching figures

## 7. Claims (PPA: informal, but include at least 1)

- Independent claim: broadest scope
- Dependent claims: add specificity

## 8. Abstract (≤150 words)

## 9. Drawings

- Black & white line drawings
- Labeled with reference numerals
